An Andhra & Telangana recipe book

Banana Leaf Kitchen

Everyday Telugu home cooking arranged around a working day: a healthy tiffin before you leave, a lunch box that travels well, and a lighter dinner. Every recipe links to the trusted source it is adapted from.

Contents

Each recipe is adapted from the linked page, mostly Swasthi's Recipes (Swasthi Shreekanth's Andhra-rooted, tested recipes), plus Vismai Food and Hebbar's Kitchen. Ingredients and servings follow the source; the method is shortened. Open the source for photos, variations and full notes.

Quantities use standard cups (240 ml) and spoons. Each card opens at the servings its source was written for; use − and + to scale. "Whistles" assume a 3–5 litre pressure cooker on medium heat.
