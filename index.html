A South Indian recipe book

Banana Leaf Kitchen

Everyday dishes from Tamil Nadu, Andhra, Karnataka and Kerala, arranged as a day of eating: tiffin in the morning, a full meals plate at lunch, and something lighter at night.

Serves
Quantities use standard measuring cups (240 ml) and spoons. Base recipes serve 4; the servings switch scales every ingredient. "Whistles" refer to a standard 3–5 litre pressure cooker on medium heat.
